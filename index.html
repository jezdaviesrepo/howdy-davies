Need to know

  • Pack a hoodie. Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon are 2,100 m up, so nights are freezing even when Scottsdale is 30°C.
  • No riding on Sunday 25. The ranch still has archery, shooting, e-bikes and the pool.
  • Drink loads of water. Desert air dries you out fast, especially on hikes.
  • Drives: Scottsdale → Flagstaff about 2.5 hrs. Flagstaff → Tucson about 4 hrs.
  • Book ahead: Lowell Observatory nights, the Boneyard tour, Pink Jeep tours and Taliesin West all sell out.
  • Shooting ranges have age and ID rules for under-18s. Call before you go.
  • Arizona doesn't change its clocks. No daylight saving here.
  • Make it an app: on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
