Leg 01 · Sat 17 – Wed 21 Oct

Scottsdale

Hilton Scottsdale Resort & Villas · 2 queen beds, pool view · room only

4 nightsWarm — 25–30°C daysCity base

For the girls & easy wins

OdySea Aquarium & Butterfly Wonderland Top pick Girls

Biggest aquarium in the Southwest with a rotating underwater tunnel, plus a walk-through butterfly conservatory right next door. Do both in one visit — about 15 min from the hotel.

Plan: half a day · book online

Desert Botanical Garden Easy stroll

Flat looping trails through saguaros and desert wildflowers in Papago Park. Go late afternoon when it cools and the light turns gold.

Plan: 2 hrs

Musical Instrument Museum Top pick Girls

In north Phoenix (~25 min). Headphones trigger each exhibit to play as you walk up — genuinely a family favourite, easy to lose a few hours in.

Plan: half a day

Resort pool day On site

The Hilton has a lazy river and waterslide. Build in at least one slow morning here between the bigger outings.

Desert & the outdoors

Sunrise desert jeep or horseback tour

Guided rides into the Sonoran Desert from north Scottsdale. Early slots are cooler and the wildlife is out — a gentle warm-up before the ranch later in the trip.

Plan: morning · pre-book

Pinnacle Peak Park Easy–moderate

A well-made out-and-back trail with big valley views and shade ramadas. Friendlier than Camelback if you want views without a scramble.

Plan: 2 hrs · go early

Hot-air balloon at sunrise Booked · Mon 19

Already on the plan for Monday morning — drifting over the desert at first light. Bring warm layers; it's chilly before the sun's up, then glorious once it rises.

Booked: Mon 19 Oct · sunrise

Town & culture

Old Town Scottsdale

Walkable blocks of galleries, Western-wear shops, sweet shops and restaurants. Easy evening wander with dinner — something for all four of you.

Taliesin West

Frank Lloyd Wright's desert home and studio. Guided tours only; book ahead. One for a cooler late-afternoon slot.

Plan: 90 min · guided

Heard Museum Girls

In Phoenix (~25 min) and widely rated the best Native American art and culture museum in the country. Beautiful courtyards, well paced for kids.

Plan: half day

Aircraft & the range

CAF Airbase Arizona Girls

Commemorative Air Force warbird hangar in Mesa (~40 min) — walk among flying WWII bombers and fighters, with ride experiences if you want to go up.

Plan: half day

Scottsdale Gun Club — full-auto

Indoor range that rents full-auto machine guns with an instructor on the lane. Minimum ages and ID rules apply and slots book up — call ahead to confirm what the whole family can do.

Note: age/ID rules · book ahead

Texas-style BBQ

Little Miss BBQ Smoked meat The one

Central-Texas-style brisket, pork ribs and sausage in Phoenix — routinely called the best BBQ in Arizona. Lunch only and it sells out, so arrive before it opens and expect a queue. Worth it.

Go: lunch · get there early

Leg 02 · Wed 21 – Sat 24 Oct

Flagstaff

Americana Motor Hotel · large room, 2 queen beds · room only · on Route 66

3 nightsHigh & cool — 2,100 mDay-trip hub

Day trips (all within easy reach)

Grand Canyon — South Rim Booked

Already on the plan — about 1.5 hrs north. Start at Mather Point, walk a stretch of the paved Rim Trail, then drive Desert View Drive for the watchtower. Wrap up warm — the rim is cold and windy in October.

Booked: full day · layers

Sedona red rocks Top pick

~45 min south. Chapel of the Holy Cross, an easy loop at Bell Rock, and a Pink Jeep tour if you want the backcountry version. Stunning and not too far.

Plan: full day

Williams & Bearizona Girls

Classic Route 66 town ~35 min west. Bearizona is a drive-through wildlife park — bears, wolves, bison from the car — then walk the baby-animal area. Grand Canyon Railway leaves from here too.

Plan: half–full day

Jerome

A former mining town clinging to a hillside, now galleries, old saloons and valley views. Quirky half-day, often paired with Sedona on the way back.

Plan: half day

In & around Flagstaff

Lowell Observatory Top pick

The evening sky program with the heated open-air seating — exactly as your notes flagged. Where Pluto was discovered; telescopes and talks after dark. Dress warm, and book tickets ahead as nights sell out.

Plan: evening · warm layers · pre-book

Downtown Flagstaff & Route 66

Historic red-brick downtown with cafés, shops and a proper Route 66 stretch — your hotel sits right on it. Good for an easy evening.

Walnut Canyon Easy

Cliff-dwelling ruins on a short canyon-rim loop, ~15 min from town. A gentle half-morning if a day-trip day needs a lighter start.

Plan: 2 hrs

Arizona Snowbowl gondola

Scenic chairlift up the San Francisco Peaks for long views and crisp mountain air. Weather-dependent in late October — check it's running.

Space, craters & aircraft

Meteor Crater Top pick Girls

The best-preserved impact crater on Earth — a mile across — about 40 min east off I-40. Rim viewpoints, a space museum and an Apollo capsule on site.

Site: meteorcrater.com · 2–3 hrs

US Naval Observatory — Flagstaff Station

The Navy's dark-sky research station west of town. It's a working facility, so public access is limited to occasional open nights — worth checking their schedule for your dates before counting on it.

Note: limited access · check ahead

Planes of Fame Air Museum, Valle

Vintage and military aircraft right on the road up to the Grand Canyon (~45 min). Easy to fold into the canyon day for an aircraft stop.

Plan: pair with Grand Canyon

Museum of Northern Arizona

Regional geology, the Colorado Plateau and Native cultures in a lovely stone building in town. A good lighter morning between big day trips.

Plan: 2 hrs

Texas-style BBQ

Bigfoot BBQ Smoked meat

Flagstaff's Texas-style smokehouse right on Route 66 — brisket, pulled pork and ribs, plus local beer. Easy walk-to dinner after a big day on the road.

Go: dinner

Proper Meats + Provisions Butcher

Downtown butcher and sandwich counter doing house-smoked brisket and pastrami. Grab lunch here to pack for the Grand Canyon or Meteor Crater drive.

Go: lunch / takeaway

Leg 03 · Sat 24 – Wed 28 Oct

White Stallion Ranch

Deluxe room · full board · all meals, all riding & ranch activities, taxes & gratuity included

4 nightsAll-inclusiveArrive = rodeo day

Included at the ranch — just show up

Horse riding The main event

Rides at every pace across a huge spread of land, matched to how confident each of you is. Note: no riding on Sunday — plan the saddle days around that.

Note: no Sunday rides

The weekly rodeo Girls

Held once a week — and your Saturday arrival lands right on it. A great, low-effort first evening while you settle in.

Archery & target shooting

Both run on the ranch and are the perfect Sunday fill-in when the horses rest. Good friendly-competition fuel for a family of four.

Fat-tyre e-bikes

Cruise the desert tracks under your own steam. Easy to do on a no-riding day or a spare afternoon.

Pool, hikes & evening entertainment Wind down

A lovely pool, walking trails straight off the property, and nightly entertainment. With full board you barely need to leave.

If you fancy a half-day off the ranch

Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum #1 on Tripadvisor Girls

Part zoo, part botanical garden, part natural-history museum — rated Tucson's #1 attraction. About 25 min away, mostly outdoors, and a brilliant family outing.

Plan: half day

Saguaro National Park (West)

Dense forests of giant saguaro cactus on a scenic loop drive with short walks. Close to the ranch and easy to combine with the Desert Museum.

Pima Air & Space Museum Top pick Girls

One of the largest aviation museums in the world — hundreds of aircraft, hangars and outdoor rows. Book the bus tour of the AMARG "Boneyard" next door, where thousands of retired military jets are parked in the desert.

Plan: half–full day · Boneyard tour books out

Full-auto range Over the archery

Beyond the ranch's own target shooting and archery, a few licensed Tucson ranges run supervised full-auto sessions with an instructor. Book a reputable one ahead and check age and ID rules for the girls.

Note: age/ID rules · book ahead

Texas-style BBQ

Brother John's Beer, Bourbon & BBQ Smoked meat

Tucson's Texas-style standout — mesquite-smoked brisket and ribs, with a big bourbon list for you and your husband. Worth a night off the ranch's full-board dinners.

Go: dinner · ranch is full board

BrushFire BBQ Co. Smoked meat

Casual, family-friendly smokehouse doing brisket, pulled pork and ribs — an easier lunch stop if you're already out at the Desert Museum or Pima.

Go: lunch

Good to know

  • Resort feeHilton Scottsdale charges ~$34.21 per room, per night, paid locally — budget roughly $137 for the four nights.
  • SundayNo horse riding at White Stallion Ranch on Sundays. Keep that day for archery, shooting, e-bikes, the pool or a desert-museum trip.
  • Pack layersScottsdale & Tucson are warm, but Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon rim sit around 2,100 m — cold mornings and evenings. Bring proper warm layers for the Lowell Observatory night.
  • DrivingScottsdale → Flagstaff ≈ 2.5 hrs (go via Sedona or stop at Montezuma Castle). Flagstaff → Tucson ≈ 4 hrs through Phoenix.
  • ClocksArizona doesn't do daylight saving, so it won't shift while you're there.
  • Book aheadBalloon rides, Taliesin West, Pink Jeep tours and the Grand Canyon Railway all sell out — reserve before you fly.
